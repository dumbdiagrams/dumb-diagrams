Dumb Diagrams turns a readable `.dgm` text file into an architecture diagram.
The engine validates the file, places resources, draws right-angle
connections, and checks the result. Studio, a local editor, lets you refine
the layout and saves your decisions in the same file.

**New here? [Make your first diagram](./quick-start/).** You need Node.js 24 or
newer. There is no account and no hosted workspace.

AWS is the most mature provider. Azure, Google Cloud, and cloud-agnostic
diagrams work but are still experimental. Dumb Diagrams is an architecture
diagram tool, not a freeform whiteboard or a live cloud inventory.
